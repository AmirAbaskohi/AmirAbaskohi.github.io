Greetings! I'm Amir, short for Amirhossein Abaskohi (امیرحسین عباسکوهی). Feel free to call me Amir 😄.

I am currently embarking on my journey as a first-year PhD student at the University of British Columbia, under the supervision of [Prof. Giuseppe Carenini](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~carenini) and [Prof. Peter West](https://peterwestai.notion.site/). I am part of the [UBC Natural Language Processing Group](https://nlp.cs.ubc.ca).

I earned my Master of Science in Computer Science from UBC with distinction (A+), supervised by [Prof. Giuseppe Carenini](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~carenini) and [Prof. Issam H. Laradji](https://issamlaradji.github.io/). My research centered on reasoning over long, multimodal documents, spanning both how such documents are represented and how they are queried. My thesis, [*Multimodal Understanding of Long Documents: From Topic Modeling to Question Answering*](https://open.library.ubc.ca/soa/cIRcle/collections/ubctheses/24/items/1.0449900), develops this in two directions: **CEMTM**, a topic modeling technique for long documents that combines contextual embeddings with cross-modal alignment, and **FM²DS**, a synthetic-data-generation pipeline for multimodal, multi-hop question answering in low-resource settings.

Prior to that, I earned my BSc in Computer Engineering from the University of Tehran. During my undergraduate studies, I had the privilege of collaborating with [Prof. Azadeh Shakeri](https://ece.ut.ac.ir/en/~shakery), [Prof. Yadollah Yaghoobzadeh](https://yyaghoobzadeh.github.io/), and [Prof. Behnam Bahrak](https://ece.ut.ac.ir/en/~bahrak/network), enriching my academic experience.

My research centers on agentic design, particularly for deep research and computer-use agents. I work across several domains on both the efficiency and the performance of these agents. Recent work includes better benchmarking of deep research agents ([`DRBench`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00172)), efficient retrieval-augmented generation systems ([`MCompassRAG`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18508) and [`SproutRAG`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18381)), and characterizing the unpredictability and randomness of different agents ([`UnpredictaBench`](https://unpredictabenchmark.github.io/)). More broadly, I remain interested in natural language inference and reasoning, long multimodal document reasoning, controlled generation, and information retrieval.

Throughout this journey, I have completed internships at **Microsoft Research**, **Huawei Research**, and **ServiceNow Research**, and collaborated with researchers at **Google**, **Meta AI**, and **Salesforce AI**. These experiences have enriched my research perspective and broadened my understanding of cutting-edge developments in natural language processing.
 
Beyond academia, I am enthusiastic about knowledge dissemination and community engagement. I share insights and discoveries through [Medium](https://medium.com/@amirhossein.abaskohi) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@amirhossein_abaskohi), fostering dialogue and knowledge exchange. I have also honed my software engineering skills through practical work, contributing to back-end development at [System Group](https://en.systemgroup.net/) in Summer 2022 and [MateMachine](https://matemachine.com/) for a year starting November 2022.
 
When I'm not working or studying, I love getting lost in the world of novels and comics 📚. They spark my imagination and give me a fun break from my busy life 🌟. I don't spend a lot of time outdoors, but I really enjoy it when I do. Living in Vancouver, with its amazing natural beauty, gives me plenty of chances to explore. Whether it's a quiet hike in the forest, a walk along the beach, or watching a colorful sunset, being in nature always makes me feel calm and refreshed. Even though life can be hectic, these little moments in nature remind me to slow down and appreciate the world around me. 🌿✨

I am happy to answer any questions and chat about research/collboration! Reach me at <a href="mailto:aabaskoh@cs.ubc.ca">aabaskoh@cs.ubc.ca</a>.
